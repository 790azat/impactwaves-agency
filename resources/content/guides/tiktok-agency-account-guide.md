Anyone can open a self-serve TikTok Ads Manager account in a few minutes. So why do so many media buyers and brands run their spend through a **TikTok agency account** instead? This guide explains what agency accounts are, what they change in practice and how to decide whether you need one.

## What is a TikTok agency account?

A TikTok agency account is an advertising account opened and managed under an official TikTok agency partner, rather than directly by the advertiser. The advertiser still runs campaigns in TikTok Ads Manager, with the same targeting, formats and reporting. The difference is the relationship behind the account: the agency has a direct line to TikTok teams and is responsible for the accounts it manages.

## Agency account vs self-serve account

| | Self-serve account | Agency account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Setup | Advertiser signs up alone | Opened through the agency partner |
| Support | Standard help center and tickets | Agency team plus a direct channel to TikTok |
| Policy questions | Answered through standard support | Agency can escalate reviews and clarify policies |
| Billing | Advertiser's own card or invoicing | Through the agency, often with more flexible top-ups |
| Guidance | Self-taught | Account audits, creative and strategy advice |

The ad platform is the same. What changes is speed of support, clarity on policy and the experience of the people behind your account.

## Who benefits most

- **Media buyers and arbitrage teams** who need stable accounts and fast answers when an ad or account is under review.
- **Brands scaling spend** that want strategic support, not only a login.
- **Advertisers new to TikTok** who want help with creative, tracking and campaign structure from day one.

If you spend small amounts occasionally, a self-serve account may be enough. Once TikTok becomes a meaningful share of your revenue, support and account stability start to matter a lot.

## What an agency account does not do

An agency account is not a way around TikTok's advertising policies. Every ad still goes through review, and repeated violations still lead to restrictions. A good agency will actually push you to be stricter, because the agency's relationship with TikTok depends on the quality of the accounts it manages.

## How to keep a TikTok ad account healthy

1. **Follow the ad policies for your vertical.** Finance, health and similar categories have extra rules. Read them before you launch.
2. **Keep landing pages consistent with your ads.** The page should deliver what the ad promises.
3. **Avoid sudden huge budget jumps** on brand new accounts. Build spend history gradually.
4. **Install the TikTok Pixel and Events API** so the algorithm gets clean conversion signals.
5. **Refresh creative often.** TikTok audiences tire of ads quickly, and fresh hooks keep CPMs down.
6. **Ask your agency before you test grey areas.** A quick question is cheaper than a restricted account.

## Creative that works on TikTok

TikTok rewards ads that look native to the feed. The best performers usually:

- Hook the viewer in the first two seconds
- Use real people, voiceover and captions rather than polished TV-style footage
- Show the product or answer quickly
- Use Spark Ads to promote organic posts that already perform

Test hooks and angles in a structured way: one variable at a time, enough budget per variant to read the result, and clear rules for when to kill or scale.
