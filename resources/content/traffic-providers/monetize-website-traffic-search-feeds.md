Display ads and affiliate links are not the only ways to earn from traffic. **Search feed monetization** lets publishers, domain owners and traffic networks earn a revenue share whenever their visitors search and click on paid listings. For the right kind of traffic, it can earn more per visitor than display, with far less clutter on the page.

## What is a search feed?

A search feed is a stream of search results and paid listings that a feed provider delivers to your pages. The ads come from large advertising networks. When a visitor clicks a paid listing, the advertiser pays, and that revenue is shared between the network, the feed provider and you.

You do not need to sign up advertisers or run an ad server. Your job is to bring real visitors with real interest and give them a good experience.

## Who can monetize traffic with search feeds

- **Content publishers** with articles on topics people research before buying
- **Domain owners** with type-in traffic to parked or premium domains
- **Ad networks and traffic platforms** with quality users to route
- **Browser extensions and apps** with a search feature, where the feed partner allows it
- **Media buyers** who buy traffic and monetize it through [search arbitrage](/guides/what-is-search-arbitrage)

## Common setups

**Related Search on Content (RSOC).** A real article with a block of related search terms. Readers who want to go deeper click a term and see a results page with ads. This is the most common setup for publishers today.

**Parked domain monetization.** A domain without its own site shows a lander with related links. Visitors who typed the domain in or followed an old link find relevant results instead of an error page.

**Search results pages.** Traffic arrives with a query, for example from a search box, and sees a results page directly.

Each feed partner has its own rules about which setups and traffic sources it accepts. Getting that match right from the start avoids problems later.

## How revenue share works

You earn a percentage of the revenue generated by your traffic. The exact share depends on the partner, your traffic volume and your traffic quality. Reports usually show:

- **Searches** made by your visitors
- **Clicks** on paid listings
- **Revenue** and **RPC** (revenue per click)

Revenue for recent days is often an estimate that can be adjusted when the advertiser network finalizes its numbers, usually because invalid clicks were filtered. Plan cash flow around final numbers. Our guide to [media buying metrics](/guides/media-buying-metrics-rpc-rpm-roi) explains how to read RPC and RPM.

## How to increase revenue per visitor

1. **Match content to commercial intent.** Topics where people are about to buy something earn more per click.
2. **Keep it relevant.** Search terms should follow naturally from the page, not be the most expensive words you can think of.
3. **Improve the page experience.** Fast pages that answer the question build trust, and trusted pages get more meaningful clicks.
4. **Segment by geography.** The same topic can earn very different amounts in the US, EU and Canada. Report by country.
5. **Protect quality.** One bad source can put the whole account at risk. See [what feed partners look for in traffic quality](/traffic-providers/traffic-quality-search-feeds).
